About

Research-driven engineering.

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, with research interests in biomedical signal processing, wearable sensing, and artificial intelligence for healthcare applications.

My research focuses on multimodal physiological signal analysis, including sleep stage classification using photoplethysmography (PPG), electrodermal activity (EDA), and accelerometer (ACC) data, as well as the development of lightweight deep learning models for robust biosignal analysis.

I am also interested in embedded AI, real-time physiological signal processing, and edge deployment of machine learning models for wearable healthcare devices. My broader research goal is to develop intelligent, efficient, and practical healthcare technologies for continuous health monitoring and decision support.

Thesis Work

Multimodal Sleep Stage Classification Using Deep Learning Models

Developing a deep learning-based methodology for automatic sleep stage classification using multimodal physiological signals by processing and analyzing PPG, EDA, and accelerometer data. The framework is designed for resource-efficient deployment in wearable and edge devices and is suitable for highly imbalanced physiological datasets.
